About

Ake
Petcu

Photographer and cinematographer

His work is, first and foremost, about people. He is interested in presence, character, and that small distance between how we want to be seen and what the camera discovers.

He studied cinematography at UNATC Bucharest. His background in cinematography naturally informs the way he photographs, through light, space, colour and atmosphere. Sometimes the image is direct and stripped back to its essentials. At other times it becomes constructed, editorial or experimental. He is less interested in perfection than in what gives an image its character.

His work with moving image, editing, colour and sound continues the same visual process.

Photography

People. Stories. Places.

Photography begins with attention: to people, light and the small moments that often go unnoticed. The work moves between portraiture, editorial photography and actor headshots, with an emphasis on character and a clear visual language.

Whether the image is direct or more constructed, light, framing and atmosphere remain central to the process.
